The questions managers ask before installing.

Will my team just mute it?
Cadence gives a non-responder a gentle nudge up to twice, then stops, so it never turns into noise people tune out. It posts on the schedule you set, not at random. The nudge is what earns replies without you asking.
Do I have to change how we run standup now?
No. Point Cadence at your existing channel and keep the questions you already ask. It runs the standup you already have, without the part where you chase people.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
You pick a plan to keep going, or Cadence pauses. There's no card on file, so nothing charges automatically. Your setup stays saved if you come back.
Can it run retros and 1:1s, or just daily standup?
Any recurring check-in that lives in Slack: weekly standup, biweekly retro, monthly team pulse. You set the questions and the rhythm.
