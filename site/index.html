Your agent isn't lazy. It's guessing.

It doesn't know what you sell, who buys it, or what they compare you to. So it ships the statistical average: the gradient, the three cards, the testimonial nobody said.

Nothing on it is wrong. Nothing on it is yours.

Three gates. Your agent can't skip one.

slopyours
brief 62/62

  1. Ask

    Five questions before a line of code: what you sell, who it's for, where visitors come from, why you. The answers become a brief you sign off.

  2. Commit

    One visual direction. One idea a competitor couldn't steal. No approved concept, no code.

  3. Check

    62 numbered checks. Failures get fixed automatically. Judgment calls come back to you.

In a hurry? Say "skip the questions." It states its assumptions, then builds.

62

C-01 to X-08 · all passed

62 checks between your agent and your users.

Zero em dashes.
Not one. The most cited tell of AI copy is gone, title tag and alt text included.
Exact colors.
One of six named directions, real hex values. An unmodified Tailwind ramp fails V-25.
Nothing invented.
No fake quotes, logos or numbers. Thin proof means a smaller claim.
The Template Test.
Put a competitor's logo on it. If it still works, it goes back to concept. V-21.

Before you star.

Isn't this just a long prompt?
A prompt asks. Deft gates: stages it can't skip, a concept you approve, 62 checks the page must pass.
Does it work with my agent?
If it reads skills. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Windsurf and 70+ others do.
Will the interview slow me down?
Five questions, one or two at a time. Or say "skip the questions" and correct its assumptions.
What does it build in?
Whatever your project already uses. Deft decides what the page says and how it looks.
Can I still have a purple gradient?
Yes. Your agent will note the trade-off once, sigh quietly, and build it.
How do I uninstall it?
npx skills remove deft, or delete the folder.
