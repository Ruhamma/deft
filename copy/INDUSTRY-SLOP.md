# Industry-Specific Slop

Read this alongside `copy/BANNED-PHRASES.md` only when the interview identified an industry. These bans stack on top of the universal list — they don't replace it.

## SaaS / B2B Tech
**Banned:** "Single pane of glass", "10x your productivity", "Enterprise-grade", "Source of truth", "Democratize [anything]", "Full-stack solution"
**Reach for:** how your users actually talk in Slack, not how a marketing team writes in decks.

## Healthcare / Telehealth
**Banned:** "Putting patients first", "Healthcare reimagined", "Your health journey", "Care when you need it most"
**Reach for:** speed, cost, access. "See a doctor in 15 minutes, $0 copay."

## Fintech / Finance
**Banned:** "Take control of your finances", "Financial freedom", "Banking reimagined", "Frictionless payments"
**Reach for:** numbers and transparency. "No hidden fees — here's our full pricing."

## E-commerce / Consumer
**Banned:** "Curated collection", "Elevate your style", "Because you deserve it", "Crafted with love"
**Reach for:** material specifics. "100% merino wool, milled in New Zealand, knit in Portland."

## AI / ML Products
**Banned:** "The power of AI", "AI-powered", "Your AI copilot", "AI-native platform"
**Reach for:** the outcome, not the technology. "Summarize a 90-minute meeting in 30 seconds."

## Creator / Design Tools
**Banned:** "Unleash your creativity", "From idea to reality", "No design skills needed"
**Reach for:** "Resize for every social platform in one click" — show the work, not the promise.

## EdTech / Learning
**Banned:** "Learn at your own pace", "Unlock your potential", "Future-proof your career"
**Reach for:** outcomes and proof. "87% of graduates get promoted within a year."

---

**New industry not listed here?** Apply the universal bans from `copy/BANNED-PHRASES.md` plus the Specificity Ladder in `copy/SKILL.md` — ask "what would this industry's practitioners actually say to each other" and write toward that, not toward the category's marketing decks.
